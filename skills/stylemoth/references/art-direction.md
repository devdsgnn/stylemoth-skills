# Art direction without a fixed house style

## Start with the subject

Identify the material the product naturally provides: code and diffs, a collection
of objects, scientific evidence, collaboration, sound, financial comparisons,
craft, editorial writing, or a physical place. Choose a visual expression that
makes that material understandable. Do not add a coding terminal to every AI
product or a decorative dashboard to every software page.

Differentiate through the relationship between content, composition, and type.
Unusual fonts, asymmetric layouts, large gaps, and bright colors are tools, not
proof of originality. A restrained interface can be distinctive through density,
proportion, real imagery, and a specific product story.

## Define the language

- **Canvas and surfaces:** determine what is open, grouped, elevated, or interactive.
- **Typography:** choose families and roles, not a list of fashionable fonts. Define
  display, body, labels, and technical text with responsive behavior.
- **Color:** establish neutral surfaces, readable text, action, selection, and
  feedback roles. Separate decorative colors from state semantics.
- **Geometry:** set gutters, content measures, spacing rhythm, component radii,
  dividers, and the occasions that deserve elevation.
- **Media:** choose real product evidence, photography, illustration, a diagram,
  a live demo, or none. State aspect ratio and crop intent.
- **Motion:** identify the state change it clarifies or the single visual moment
  it supports. Do not prescribe a reveal effect to every section.

Keep the most memorable gesture specific and controlled. A strong product image,
type composition, or purposeful interaction can lead; everything else need not
compete with it. Preserve user-specified choices even when they resemble a familiar
style. Avoid blanket bans on legitimate palettes, fonts, or layouts.

## Compose the first viewport and the continuation

Choose what the visitor should understand first and what the next visible content
promises. Balance headline, evidence, navigation, and action at real laptop heights.
Neither crowding every capability above the fold nor filling a viewport with empty
space is automatically good.

Vary later sections by their function: explanation, comparison, demonstration,
proof, workflow, or decision. Keep continuity through shared type, color roles,
gutters, and media treatment. Do not force alternation merely for variety or make
every section the same centered heading plus three equal cards.

## Exploration and fidelity

When options are requested, keep the product content and task comparable across
them. Show a structural difference, not just blue versus purple. Explain which
audience or use case each direction favors. Apply the selected direction rather
than merging every option afterward.

For direct implementation, a compact direction note may be sufficient. Use a
mockup or generated bitmap when it resolves ambiguity or supplies imagery. Do not
make an image-generation service a prerequisite for normal UI work.

During implementation, preserve defining decisions: white versus warm canvas,
compact versus spacious density, product-led versus image-led composition, and
the chosen container structure. A responsive adaptation changes arrangement;
it should not accidentally become a new unrelated visual identity.
